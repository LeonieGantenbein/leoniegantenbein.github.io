CLEAR Labelling AI Involvement in Texts

Conception · Language · Evidence · Attribution · Responsibility

Leonie Gantenbein · Version 1.0 · 2026 · Licence CC BY 4.0

Overview

CLEAR is a proposal for voluntarily disclosing in what respects and to what extent an artificial intelligence was involved in a text – and who stands behind it. Instead of a blanket yes or no (“AI-generated”), CLEAR provides five statements for each person involved, comparable to a citation: as information about the craft.

Culture first, then labelling. A label alone is not enough. As long as disclosure is penalised, only those who can afford it will label, and silence remains the rational choice. CLEAR presupposes a cultural negotiation that has not yet taken place. Chapter “Culture before Labelling”

Quick reference

AxisMeaningLevels
C – ConceptionConception, line of thoughtC0 purely human · C1 AI as discussion partner · C2 individual content parts by AI · C3 content comprehensively generated by AI
L – LanguageLinguistic editingL0 without AI · L1 proofreading · L2 stylistic editing · L3 substantive editing (structure, argument) · L4 translation
E – EvidenceSources on which the text restsE0 without AI · E1 source access · E2 source processing · E3 source interpretation · E4 interpretation of results; suffix (p) published / (n) unpublished
A – AttributionDeclarationAI models used and time period
R – ResponsibilityAccountabilityR1 fully checked and taken responsibility for · R2 spot-checked · R3 unchecked, no one responsible

Only the highest level reached is given for each axis. Each person involved (author, editor, translator …) gets a line of their own. The label appears before or after the text.

Example

CLEAR (author): C1, L3, E(p)1, A: Claude Opus 4.8, Fable 5, Opus 5, 04–09/2026, R1

Full description of all levels, guidance on classification and use in educational institutions: chapter “Specification”

Status

CLEAR has not yet been tested or evaluated in practice and is certainly incomplete. Feedback, experience from use and proposals for images, video and code are welcome – via issues in the GitHub repository.

Origin

CLEAR comes from the book Zwischen Prompt und Welt. Über das Leben mit KI [Between Prompt and World: On Living with AI] by Leonie Gantenbein (forthcoming December 2026, Schwabe Verlag) and builds on concepts from Annetzung. Handeln durch Technologie und Imagination [Acting through Technology and Imagination] (2025), in particular the concept of world transparency (Welttransparenz).
